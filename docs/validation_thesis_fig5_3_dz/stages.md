# Stage by stage: Fortran vs C++ CPU vs C++ GPU

Every item of the comparison, in the order the programs execute. The summary and the argument are
in [README.md](README.md); criteria, prediction and their changes in
[scripts/criteria.md](scripts/criteria.md). All numbers and tables are generated by the scripts in
[scripts/](scripts/) from the raw run files (table fragments between `BEGIN/END table` markers).

**Notation.** (p) printed precision: a Fortran value as printed (e.g. `0.4661086D-03`, 7 digits)
against the C++ value at full precision; the tolerance is 1.01 × ½ unit of the last printed digit.
(f) full precision on both sides. Terms (BK coefficient, replay, specular grid, kinetic block):
[README](README.md#verdict), "Terms used in this report". Verdict words: **identical** (bitwise or equal integers/text),
**rounding** (full precision, within criterion), **printed** (within printed precision),
**explained** (outside, with a quantitative explanation), **FINDING** (unexplained; none),
**shared code** (CPU↔GPU rows that run the same host code), **n/a**. "C++" without qualifier is
the CPU run; the GPU run's set-up output is identical to it (item 1).

(4–6) · [Propagation](#block-3-propagation-set-up-and-propagation) (7–13) ·

### 4. Is the potential the same, on the full grid?

**Answer.** Yes: at identical physical points the Fortran's stored potential and the C++ PES differ
by at most 2.8e-14 of max(|V|, 1 eV) over all 85.5 million stored values; as used by the
propagations (each code through its own symmetry maps, 648 million full-grid values) by at most
7.5e-14, which is the size of the PES's own asymmetry on this grid (7.3e-14 / 7.9e-14 with the
C++ / Fortran maps). The runs do not write V; the helper's V from `build-fast` and
`build-cuda-fast` is bitwise identical. Verdict: **rounding** (criterion 1e-13); CPU↔GPU
**identical** (helper).

**Compared.** Fortran `.pes` (V in hartree before the −dt factor, symmetry-reduced; symmetryIndex
expanded) against two helper outputs computed with the library of the run: the stored potential
as the run builds it (C++ representatives) and the PES evaluated at every full-grid point without
symmetry. The two codes use different symmetry representatives (5 of 19 (x, y) points in common;
documented deviation), hence the separate "same point" and "as used" rows.

**Why |ψ(k)| at k = 0 and at the grid edge differ.** These values come from an FFT of the Gaussian
on the specular grid: 256 terms of size up to ~1 that cancel to 1e-10. At k = 0 the DFT factor is 1
and at the edge it is (−1)<sup>j</sup>, so the correctly rounded DFT of the double-precision samples can
be computed with `math.fsum`. With the C++ α and k<sub>Z</sub> it equals the C++ value to 2e-17 (an
independent numpy FFT gives the same); with the Fortran's own α and k<sub>Z</sub> (quad-precision site,
docs/fortran_deviations.md) it reproduces the Fortran's printed values within their half unit. The
cause is the different rounding of the sample phases k<sub>Z</sub>·Z (one ulp of k<sub>Z</sub>·Z₀ is 2.8e-14
rad), amplified by the 10-order cancellation; it has no effect on the propagation.

**Symmetry between diffraction channels.** For H₂(j = 0) at normal incidence on a C6v surface,
channels (n, m) related by the 12 operations should have equal probabilities (summed over
m<sub>J</sub>). Only the mirror (n, m) ↔ (m, n) holds (median 2.6e-14 relative in the C++ CPU run); every
other operation, the rotations C2, C3 and C6 included, is broken by a median 2.5–3.9% (p95
46–59%), identically in all three codes (orbit spreads agree to 1.4e-9). That mirror is the only
operation that maps the parallelogram of FFT momenta in (x, y) (n, m = −6…5 on the 12 × 12 grid)
onto itself. The kinetic operator T<sub>xy</sub> on that grid is therefore not C6v-symmetric, so the
breaking comes from the dynamics on this grid (both programs also warn that the diffraction basis is
too small for the open channels). A larger (x, y) grid would test this; it is a convergence
question of the input. A bug common to both codes would look the same, and a port comparison cannot
see it. P_reaction at 0.40–0.50 eV is 2.5e-4…5.2e-4 where the thesis curve is ~0 (1 − ΣP also
collects norm still on the grid at T, 1.2e-4, and unanalysed channels); the same in all codes.
